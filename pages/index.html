Slow Clocks, Bent Light

A heavy mass, a light mass, a far observer, a distant light that flashes once a second, and you. Every one of them keeps its own time. Fly around, follow any of the clocks, and watch light bend.

Enter

W A S D to fly, drag to look, wheel to zoom. On a phone: left thumb flies, right thumb looks.

How sure is this

The page was tested with numbers against the textbook formulas. Then the project went further: several of its formulas, and the theory behind them, are now proved by machine.

TestedClock rates, free fall, round orbits and bent light agree with the textbook sums, light bending to about one part in a hundred and the rest to five digits.
ProvedLean, a proof checker, confirms every step: Schwarzschild's space-time is empty, it is the only round empty one, and the clock rates and orbits follow from it.
Not claimedAn exact formula for two masses circling each other. There almost certainly is none.
