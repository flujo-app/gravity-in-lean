Slow Light Town

Here light crawls along at walking pace, and a day lasts one minute. Nothing else about the place is unusual.

W A S D to move, mouse to look, Shift to sprint, Space to rise. On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks.

Getting around

  • W A S D or arrows to move, mouse to look (click the view first).
  • Shift to sprint. Mouse wheel changes your pace.
  • Space or E to rise, Q to sink.
  • N switches between Eyes and Now. C turns the colour shift off and on.
  • R takes you back to where you started.

Eyes and Now

Eyes is what actually reaches you: old light, older the further it has come. Now is where everything is at this instant by your own reckoning, which nobody can see.

What's real

The bending and bunching of the town when you move, the direction of the colour shift, your slow watch, the oval ripples of the beacon's light, the hole bending the view and slowing clocks, and the visitor as a slice of a four-dimensional cube all follow the real rules. Invented: light at walking pace, the one-minute day, the hole's size and its pull on the towers, and the colours beyond red and violet.
